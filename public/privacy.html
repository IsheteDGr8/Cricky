Privacy policy

Cricky is a cricket scoring app. This page applies to the website and the iOS/Android apps.

What we store

  • Tournament, team and player names that an admin types in.
  • Ball-by-ball match events and derived scorecards.
  • For admins: a Google (or later Apple) sign-in. We keep the Firebase Auth user id and the role in the database.
  • For scorers: an anonymous session plus a per-match code. We do not ask for an email.
  • Viewers do not sign in.

We do not sell data, show ads, or collect precise location.

Who can see it

Anyone with a match or tournament link can read scores. Only that match’s scorers and admins can write. Scorer codes are visible only to admins.

Delete your account

If you signed in (admin or scorer), open More in the app and choose Delete my account. That removes your Firebase Auth user. Match events you already wrote stay in the scorecard (they are part of the public record of the game).

Contact

Report a security issue through private vulnerability reporting on GitHub.
